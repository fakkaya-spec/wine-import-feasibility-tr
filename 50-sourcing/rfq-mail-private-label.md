# ✉️ GÖNDERİLECEK METİN — BURADAN AŞAĞISI KOPYALANIR

**Subject:** Wine supply to Türkiye — private label enquiry — `<COMPANY>`

---

Dear `<CONTACT NAME / Export Sales Team>`,

We are a company based in Türkiye evaluating a long-term import programme for
still wine in the price-performance segment of the Turkish market, and are
building a shortlist of producers able to bottle under our own brand.

Our target is a **750 ml still dry white**, value/entry export tier —
Chardonnay, Colombard-Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Verdejo, Airén-Chardonnay,
Trebbiano, or an equivalent white blend you would recommend. Please quote **two
quality levels**: (1) your cheapest commercially acceptable, export-ready white,
and (2) the best-value option one level above.

Please do not quote a single volume. We need a price per bottle at **5,000 /
10,000 / 25,000 / 50,000 bottles per year and one full 20' container**, with
**EXW and FOB stated separately** — EXW naming the place, FOB the port — plus
currency, price per case, MOQ, Incoterm and validity date.

For private label, please state: your **private label MOQ**; which of wine,
bottle, closure, front and back label and carton are **included** in the price
and which are extra; **artwork set-up** and **plate/cutting-die** charges;
whether you can apply a **Turkish-language back label at your own facility**;
lead time, and how much private label adds to it; **sample cost** and whether a
**pre-production sample run** is possible; any annual minimum; and whether
**exclusivity for Türkiye** is available.

We also need the packaging figures we cannot calculate a delivered cost
without: **empty and filled bottle weight, bottle dimensions, bottles per case,
case gross weight and external dimensions, cases per pallet, bottles per pallet
and pallet dimensions.**

**`[P]`** Please confirm which **preferential origin document** you can issue for
shipments to Türkiye — a **EUR.1 movement certificate** or an **invoice
declaration**. Write the document name; *"yes, we export"* does not answer this.
Please also confirm that the wine is **wholly produced and bottled in your
country** with no imported bulk component, whether you would **commit
contractually to issuing it for every shipment**, and **from which country's
port** the goods will leave.

**`[N]`** Please confirm whether you can issue a **non-preferential certificate
of origin**, that the wine is wholly produced and bottled in your country, and
from which country's port the goods will leave.

The attached **response sheet** sets this out in the order we compare offers,
and is the fastest route to a comparable quotation.

We would be grateful for your reply by `<DEADLINE>`.

Kind regards,

`<NAME>`
`<POSITION>`
`<COMPANY>`
`<ADDRESS>`
`<EMAIL>` · `<PHONE>`
